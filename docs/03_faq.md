# Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a patch test?**
Yes, for any colour service. Come in at least 48 hours before your appointment for a quick, free patch test (about 5 minutes, no booking needed during opening hours). If you have had a patch test with us in the last 6 months and nothing has changed, you don't need another one. Without a patch test we cannot colour your hair, and the late cancellation rules apply.

**I'm pregnant. Can I still have my hair coloured?**
Many clients do, but we always recommend speaking to your midwife or GP first. We can also suggest gentler options such as highlights that don't touch the scalp.

**Do you cut curly or afro hair?**
Yes. Two of our stylists, Amara and Jess, specialise in curly, coily and afro hair. Mention your hair type when booking so we pick the right stylist.

**Can I bring my dog?**
Only assistance dogs, sorry.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?**
Yes, free Wi-Fi. Ask at reception for the password.

**Do you sell hair products?**
Yes, we stock professional shampoos, conditioners and styling products. Existing clients get 15% off products bought on the day of their appointment.

**What if I'm not happy with my haircut?**
Tell us within 7 days and we'll fix it free of charge. We don't give refunds on services, but we will always put it right.

**Do you offer refunds on products?**
Unopened products can be returned within 14 days with a receipt for a full refund.

**How do I pay?**
Card, contactless, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash. We don't accept cheques. Gift vouchers can be used towards any service or product.
