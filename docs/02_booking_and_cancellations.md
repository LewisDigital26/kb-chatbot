# Booking and cancellation policy

## How to book
- Online at any time through our booking page.
- By phone on 024 7496 0123 during opening hours.
- In person at the salon.
We do not take bookings through Instagram or Facebook messages.

## Deposits
- A £20 deposit is needed for any appointment over £100 and for all colour corrections.
- The deposit comes off your final bill.
- Deposits are not needed for cuts, blow-dries or treatments under £100.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment
- Free if you give at least 24 hours' notice.
- Less than 24 hours' notice: you lose your deposit, or pay 50% of the service price if you had no deposit.
- No-shows are charged 100% of the service price before we can book you again.
- You can move an appointment once for free with 24 hours' notice.

## Lateness
If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten your service or rebook you, and the late cancellation rules apply.

## Children
Under-16s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the whole appointment.
We do not colour or chemically treat the hair of anyone under 16.
