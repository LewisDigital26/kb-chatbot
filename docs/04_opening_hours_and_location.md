## Where we are
The Old Print Works (fictional address), Coventry city centre.
About 5 minutes' walk from Pool Meadow bus station.

## Parking
There is no parking at the salon. The nearest car park is a 3-minute walk away. We don't refund parking.

## Accessibility
The salon is on the ground floor with step-free access and an accessible toilet. We have one height-adjustable chair; mention it when booking if you need it.
