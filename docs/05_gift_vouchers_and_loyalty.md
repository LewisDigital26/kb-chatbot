# Gift vouchers and loyalty scheme

## Gift vouchers
- Available for any amount from £10, in the salon or by phone.
- Valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
- Can be used for services and products.
- Cannot be exchanged for cash, and no change is given; any balance stays on the voucher until it expires.
- Lost vouchers can only be replaced if you still have the receipt.

## Loyalty scheme: Bloom Points
- Earn 1 point for every £1 spent on services (not products or gift vouchers).
- 100 points = £10 off a future service.
- Points expire if you don't visit for 12 months.
- Sign up for free at reception or when booking online.

## Refer a friend
When a new client you refer finishes their first appointment, you both get £5 off your next service.
Your friend must mention your name when they book.
